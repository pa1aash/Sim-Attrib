## 0. THE PRE-REGISTERED RULE, ITS TRIGGER, AND THE PART OF IT THAT TURNED OUT TO BE WRONG

**This section is first because it is the one that most deserves suspicion: a rule written in
advance fired against the project, and a measurement taken afterwards is being used to
qualify it.** Reporting it anywhere but the top would be hiding it.

**What was written in advance.** `src/diagnostics/k6_spectrum.py`, committed at `a8159f8`
**before this run produced a number**:

> A six-column rank deficiency can arise two ways, and they mean opposite things.
> **within-mechanism** — a near-null direction supported on `base:X` and `adv:X` for a single
> mechanism `X`. That says two different deformations of the SAME component are hard to tell
> apart. It is not a threat to component attribution … **cross-mechanism** — a near-null
> direction mixing two different mechanisms. That IS a threat to component attribution …
> **A six-column INSEPARABLE verdict whose null directions are all within-mechanism does not
> overturn the three-column result; one with a cross-mechanism null direction does.** That
> distinction is stated here, before the numbers, so it cannot be chosen afterwards to suit
> them.

**What fired.** Both of `S_B`'s six-column near-null directions are **cross-mechanism**,
mixing progression and observation, with `|v_k|` stable across the entire plateau and **no
borderline members**. The classification used the pre-registered `vk_min = 0.3` rule of
`docs/THRESHOLDS.md` §2.1 and no new threshold. **The rule's trigger fired in the direction
against the project, and it is recorded as having fired.**

**What the rule got right, and what it got wrong.** The rule has two parts, and they have
different standing now:

- **The classification criterion is correct and did its job.** It was written to separate a
  trivial six-column failure from a real component confound. The failure is a real component
  confound. Nothing about that is being reinterpreted.
- **The predicted *implication* — that a cross-mechanism confound at `K = 6` overturns the
  `K = 3` result — was a guess about a consequence, and it has since been measured
  directly, and it is false.** All eight three-column family assignments separate. The
  six-column null direction places weight on `base:progression`, `adversarial:progression`
  **and** `adversarial:observation` at once — that is the progression component carrying *two*
  distortion parameters simultaneously, which no one-family-per-component model permits. The
  confound is real and it lives in a space no declarable family assignment reaches.

**The honest characterisation of what happened.** A pre-registered *criterion* fired and is
reported as it fired. A pre-registered *prediction* about what that firing would imply was
then tested exhaustively — eight assignments, not a sample — and refuted. Refining a
prediction against a direct measurement is a different act from reinterpreting a criterion to
suit a result, but **it is an act in the project's favour made after seeing the data, and it
should be weighed as one.** `DEVIATIONS.md` **D-13** records it.

**What would have made this unnecessary.** The rule should have said what it now says: that a
cross-mechanism six-column confound bears on the three-column result *only if it lies inside
some three-column assignment*, which is a testable condition rather than an assumed one. It
was not written that way because the eight-assignment test had not been thought of when the
rule was written. That is the same failure mode `DEVIATIONS.md` **D-9** records for the
adversarial family set: **the right check was available earlier than it was run.**

## 2. `S_B` AT `K = 6` — the unfavourable finding, and why the estimator is not the explanation

### 2.1 There is exactly one six-column object, and the brief assumed there were two

The session brief asks for the six-column spectrum *"under (a) the base distortion families
(b) the adversarial families"*. **Those are not two objects.** A family set supplies three
columns; the union of the two declared sets is one matrix, the same one whichever set is named
first. Manufacturing a second would mean inventing three further families with the results
already visible — `DEVIATIONS.md` **D-9**'s problem at twice the size, in a much larger search
space. `DEVIATIONS.md` **D-12** records the departure and what was done instead.

### 2.2 The verdict, and the three things that rule out the easy explanations

| | `S_B`, `K = 3` base | `S_B`, `K = 3` adversarial | `S_B`, `K = 6` union |
|---|---|---|---|
| singular values | 27.80, 5.208, 2.747 | 63.10, 3.639, 0.9765 | 68.13, 11.50, 4.679, 2.642, 0.6250, 0.1083 |
| spread (decades) | 1.01 | 1.81 | **2.80** |
| gap prominence | 1.48 | 1.65 | **1.40** |
| where `τ·σ₁` sits | below the whole spectrum | below the whole spectrum | **inside, between `σ₄` and `σ₅`** |
| rank at `τ` | 3/3 | 3/3 | **4/6** |
| `κ` | 10.12 | 64.62 | **628.9** |
| verdict | separable | separable | **INSEPARABLE** |

**It is not an unresolved estimator.** Every one of the six singular values is **resolved**
under the pre-registered criterion — the largest variation factor across the h-plateau is
**1.023**, against `docs/THRESHOLDS.md` §1.4's factor of 2. The rank is *determined*: 4, not
an interval. `results/robustness/wide_spectrum_check.yaml`, which is where this number came
from before this session, was computed at a **single step size**, which this project's own
§1.4 says *"is not a result"*. It now has the plateau, the resolution test and the
equivalence-class stability requirement, and it survives all three.

**It is not a threshold artefact.** The verdict is INSEPARABLE across `τ` from **0.005 to
1.0** — the whole upper end of a four-decade sweep. It becomes separable only at `τ ≤ 0.001`,
a tenfold loosening, and at that setting `κ_max` rises to 1000 while the measured `κ` is
628.9: on `docs/THRESHOLDS.md` §1.2's own pricing table, separation would then cost
`κ² ≈ 4×10⁵` replicates, which sits between that table's *"affordable but expensive"* and
*"not affordable"* rows. **There is no tolerance at which the six-column object is both
separable and cheap.**

**It is not structural.** `S_B` has `d = 10 ≥ 6`, so no singular value is zero by
construction; `n_structurally_zero = 0`. `S_A` cannot be tested this way at all — `d = 4 < 6`
forces two structural zeros and its six-column `κ` is `inf` for reasons having nothing to do
with the question.

### 2.3 What is actually confounded, and it is interpretable

Both near-null directions are **cross-mechanism**, and both name progression and observation:

| `σ` | `σ/σ₁` | class members | mechanism energy (transmission / progression / observation) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.6250 | 0.009174 | `base:progression`, `adversarial:progression`, `adversarial:observation` | 0.043 / 0.595 / 0.362 |
| 0.1083 | 0.001590 | `base:progression`, `adversarial:progression`, `adversarial:observation` | 0.045 / 0.333 / 0.622 |

The first direction reads, at the representative `h`, `−0.678` on `base:progression`, `−0.367`
on `adversarial:progression` and `−0.600` on `adversarial:observation`, with every `|v_k|`
stable to three decimal places across the plateau.

**In words: a drifting removal hazard is nearly indistinguishable from a constant hazard
change combined with a drifting reporting rate.** That is not a contrivance — it is the
confound `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` **Q-12** predicted in prose before any of these numbers
existed, and it is an epidemiological commonplace. It is also exactly why it does **not** reach
a three-column model: it needs the progression component to carry two distortion parameters at
once, and a one-parameter family cannot.

### 2.4 The gapless objection, now measured with the machinery rather than argued

`audit/R2_THREAT_CHECK.md` §1.3 records Gutenkunst et al. (2007): sensitivity spectra for
models of this class are *"spread roughly uniformly over many decades, with no gap"*, and if
so, *"numerical rank at tolerance τ"* reports where the analyst put `τ`.

**At `K = 6` that picture describes `S_B` well.** The spectrum spans 2.80 decades; its gap
prominence — the largest adjacent ratio divided by the median — is **1.40**, i.e. the decay is
close to geometric with no break anywhere; and `τ·σ₁` falls **inside** it. At `K = 3` under
either declared set the spread is about a decade and `τ·σ₁` sits below every singular value.
**The reassurance G4 reported is confirmed as a fact about `K = 3`, and the objection's
mechanism is confirmed as present once the list is longer.**

**One safeguard did not do the job it was nominated for, and this is worth stating.**
`audit/R2_THREAT_CHECK.md` §1.3 proposed that a genuinely gapless spectrum *"should surface"*
in the resolution test, because an unresolved singular value must be counted toward the rank
in neither direction. **It did not surface there.** All six values are resolved to within
2.3%. The resolution test measures whether the *estimator has converged*; spectral density is
a property of the *matrix*. They are independent, and a dense spectrum that is estimated
perfectly passes the test while being exactly the situation the test was hoped to catch. This
is not a vacuous flag — the resolution test can and does fail on unconverged estimates — but
it is a safeguard pointed at the wrong quantity, and `R2_THREAT_CHECK.md`'s sentence should
not be relied on again.

## 4. THE `κ_max` BRANCH — resolved, not deferred a second time

G4 finding 1.4 established that at the registered pair the two criteria of
`docs/THRESHOLDS.md` §1.3 are one criterion, because `κ_max` was *defined* as `1/τ`, and that
the `κ` branch is therefore unreachable there. The brief required this be resolved or the
blocking dependency named. **It is resolved.**

**The closed form.** With every singular value resolved, the rank branch fires iff `κ > 1/τ`
and the `κ` branch fires iff `κ > κ_max`. So the `κ` branch fires **alone** exactly on

    κ_max  <  κ  ≤  1/τ

which is **empty whenever `κ_max ≥ 1/τ`** — and the registered pair sets `κ_max = 1/τ`
precisely. There is no `(τ, κ_max)` with `κ_max = 1/τ` at which the `κ` branch can fire alone,
for any `κ` whatsoever.

**The check that could have failed.** The verdict was recomputed through the production
`analyse()` at every point of a 9 × 12 grid over `τ` and `κ_max` independently — 108 points per
spectrum — and each row checked against the closed form. **The algebra and the production rule
agree at every point**, for every summary set and every column count. `tests/test_k6_spectrum.py`
feeds the same checker a grid with the ceiling comparison inverted, and a grid with the rank
rule stated on the wrong singular value, and requires it to catch both — so a clean result here
is a measurement rather than a tautology.

**Where the branch is reachable, concretely.** For `S_B`'s six-column spectrum at `κ = 628.9`,
holding `κ_max = 100` and taking `τ ∈ {10⁻⁴, 10⁻³}` gives rank 6/6 — full column rank — with
the verdict still INSEPARABLE **on the condition-number criterion alone**. That is the first
time in this project's history the `κ` branch has decided anything, and it required breaking
the `κ_max = 1/τ` tie the thresholds registered.

**The consequence for reporting, which is a correction G4 already asked for.** `κ_max` and `τ`
are one threshold with two names at the registered pair, and results files, `SUMMARY_TABLE.md`
and the verdict string all present them as two checks. That reporting defect is unchanged by
this session and remains open.

## 5. WHAT THIS CHECK DOES NOT CERTIFY

- **That the eight assignments are a sample of anything.** They are every combination of *two*
  family sets this project chose, one of which was designed to fail and both of which are its
  own. Eight points drawn from two sets is not evidence about distortion families in general,
  and **Q-13 is narrowed, not closed.**
- **That `S_B` is safe at two parameters per component.** It is not: that is finding 2, and it
  is the unfavourable half of this document. What is established is only that no *declarable*
  one-family-per-component model reaches the confound.
- **That the `K = 6` failure would not appear at `K = 4` or `K = 5`.** Only 3 and 6 were
  measured. The intermediate cases — one component carrying two parameters while the others
  carry one — are constructible from the columns already recorded in
  `results/robustness/k6_spectrum.yaml` and **were not run**.
- **That refining the pre-registered implication in §0 was the right call.** It is a judgement
  made after seeing data, in the project's favour, and both readings are laid out so the
  operator can take the other one. **P-2.**
- **That this was an independent review.** It is not. Same project, same code, same session
  that wrote the check — the identical limitation `GATES.md` G4 records, unchanged.
- **That the base parameter point is representative.** Every Jacobian here linearises about one
  `θ`, as every Jacobian in this repository does. `results/cost_gate.yaml` does not exist and
  `p_sel` is still unmeasured, so nothing here says what happens elsewhere in `Ω₀`.
- **That `R = 128` suffices for the columns.** It was chosen in G3 for the plateau and is
  carried unchanged so this run is comparable with the recorded ones. Nobody has checked it
  against a larger `R`.
